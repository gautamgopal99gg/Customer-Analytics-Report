# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to help organizations understand employee attrition patterns and workforce dynamics. By transforming raw HR data into actionable insights, this dashboard enables HR teams and business leaders to make informed decisions regarding employee retention, engagement, and workforce planning.

## Problem Statement

Employee attrition can significantly impact organizational performance, productivity, and recruitment costs. This project aims to identify key factors influencing employee turnover by analyzing employee demographics, job satisfaction, departmental performance, and educational backgrounds.

## Dashboard Highlights

### Workforce Overview

* Total Employees: 1,470
* Active Employees: 1,233
* Attrition Count: 237

* Average Employee Age: 37 Years

* Employee distribution by age bands

### Job Satisfaction Insights

* Satisfaction ratings across job roles
* Comparative analysis of employee engagement
* Identification of roles with potential retention concerns

## Key Insights

* The Research & Development department accounts for the highest proportion of employee attrition.
* Employees in younger age groups demonstrate relatively higher turnover rates.
* Life Sciences and Medical education backgrounds contribute significantly to overall attrition.
* Job satisfaction levels vary across different roles, highlighting opportunities for targeted HR interventions.
